Notes in simpleTracker come in two types: **text notes** (freeform Markdown) and **list notes** (checklists). Here's how to use both.

## Creating a Note

1. Open simpleTracker and navigate to the **Notes** tab
2. Tap the **+** button to create a new note
3. Choose your note type:
   - **Text** — freeform writing with full Markdown support
   - **List** — a checklist of items you can mark complete

<Image src={notesPage} alt="Notes page showing a list of text and list notes" width={300} class="guide-screenshot" />

## Writing with Markdown

Text notes support full Markdown including:

- **Bold**, *italic*, and ~~strikethrough~~
- Headings (# H1, ## H2, ### H3)
- Bullet and numbered lists
- Links and code blocks

The formatting toolbar gives you quick access to common styles, or you can type Markdown syntax directly. A live preview shows how your note will look.

<Image src={noteDetailsNote} alt="Text note with Markdown editing and preview" width={300} class="guide-screenshot" />

List notes work as simple checklists — tap items to mark them complete:

<Image src={noteDetailsList} alt="List note with checkable items" width={300} class="guide-screenshot" />

## Organizing Notes

- **Pin** important notes to keep them at the top of your list
- **Archive** old notes you don't need day-to-day (they're still accessible, just hidden from the main view)
- **Assign to a project** to group related notes together

Use the search bar to find notes quickly, or filter by project:

## Sharing Notes

To share a note with another user:

1. Open the note you want to share
2. Tap the share/collaborate option
3. The other user needs to be in your contacts (exchanged via QR code)

Shared notes sync in real time — both users see changes instantly.
