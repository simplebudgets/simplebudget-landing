Tasks in simpleTracker are designed for things you need to do — with subtasks, due dates, and optional recurrence to keep you on track.

## Creating a Task

1. Navigate to the **Tasks** tab
2. Tap the **+** button
3. Give your task a title and optionally a description
4. Set a **due date** if it has a deadline
5. Assign it to a **project** if relevant

## Adding Subtasks

Break larger tasks into smaller steps:

1. Open a task
2. Add subtasks (up to 50 per task)
3. Check off subtasks as you complete them
4. The parent task shows progress based on subtask completion

## Due Dates

Tasks with due dates show visual indicators:

- 🔴 **Overdue** — past the due date
- 🟡 **Due today** — needs attention now
- 🔵 **Due tomorrow** — coming up next

## Recurring Tasks

For tasks that repeat on a schedule:

1. Open a task and enable **recurring**
2. Set the interval: every N days, weeks, or months
3. Choose the **anchor**:
   - **Due date** — the next occurrence is calculated from the due date
   - **Completion date** — the next occurrence is calculated from when you actually finish it

When you complete a recurring task, a new instance is automatically created based on your settings.

## Searching and Filtering

Use the search bar at the top of the Tasks tab to find tasks by title. Filter by completion status to focus on active work or review what you've accomplished.
